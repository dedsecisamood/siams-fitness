Read the signs

How you'll know
it's working.

On track

  • 01Lifts go up a little most weeks
  • 02Waist shrinks 0.5–1 cm a month
  • 03You sleep well and wake up rested
  • 04Soreness lasts a day or two, not five
  • 05You look forward to training days
  • 06Shirts get tight on the shoulders, loose at the waist

Stop and fix

  • 01Sharp pain in a joint (a dull muscle burn is fine)
  • 02Strength dropping two weeks in a row
  • 03Losing more than 1 kg a week
  • 04Dreading every session and feeling flat for days
  • 05Ego lifting: swinging, bouncing, half reps for the camera
  • 06Missing two days in a row
Real questions

Asked and
answered.

Is it really free?
Yes. Free, no ads, no catch. We're testing with a small crew right now, so it gets better every week. Just like you.
How is my plan made?
From your answers: how many days you can train, for how long, where, your experience, any injuries and what you eat. Every day uses proven moves for your equipment, hits each muscle about twice a week, and starts easy so you learn the form first.
No protein powder? For real?
For real. Eggs, dal, chola, soya chunks, chicken, fish, milk, oats and peanuts get most people to their target. Ten recipes, 16 to 34 g of protein each, filtered for what you eat.
I missed a day. Now what?
Nothing dramatic. Do the next session in order the next time you train. Never cram two into one day. Miss once, that's life. Miss twice, that's a habit. That's the line.
Who can see my data?
Only you. You log in with your own username and PIN, and your plan and logs live in your account. Delete everything any time in Settings.
I forgot my PIN.
Ask Siam to reset it. There's no email on your account, so it's done by hand.
I have an injury. Is this safe?
Tell us in the questions and your plan skips the riskiest moves for that area. Better 1% is general fitness guidance, not medical advice. If something hurts, stop, and see a doctor or physio.
How do I put it on my phone?
Open the app in Safari or Chrome and choose Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen like a real app, and your logs sync to your account on every device.
